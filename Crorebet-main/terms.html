5. How To Use the Help Center — Step by Step

Follow these steps to get your issue resolved correctly:

  1. Open the Help Center home page Browse the problem categories shown on the main page and pick the one that matches your issue.
  2. Select your issue type Choose Deposit Problem, Withdrawal Problem or Email Verification.
  3. Prepare your details Keep your registered email address, transaction ID (if applicable) and a clear screenshot ready before contacting support.
  4. Submit your request Describe the issue briefly and precisely — include the date, amount and any reference numbers.
  5. Do not send duplicate requests Wait for the team to reply to your existing request; duplicates slow down resolution for everyone.
  6. Cooperate with verification If the support team asks for clarification or additional proof, respond promptly so the issue can be closed quickly.
